🔄 Hangout Posts — RTDB → Firestore Migration

Step 1 — Migrate: Copies posts from Realtime Database to Firestore. Posts already in Firestore are intelligently merged (new comments/reactions are added; existing data is never overwritten).

Step 2 — Clean Up: After migration is confirmed complete, deletes posts from RTDB — but only after verifying each one exists safely in Firestore.
